Deadlines and guidelines

Published by the Academic Coordinator. All times are 11:59 pm on the due date.

What do Hard and Soft deadlines mean?
Hard
Submission closes at the due date. After that you can only submit if the coordinator grants you an extension.
Soft
You can still submit after the due date, but the submission is marked Late.

Guidelines

Final report formatting guide

Published 1 Mar 2027

Use the department template, A4, 12 pt body text and Harvard referencing. The main text should be 10,000–12,000 words, not counting appendices. Submit one PDF or DOCX file.

Download final_report_formatting_guide.pdf (312 KB)

Progress report guidance

Published 8 Jan 2027

Keep each report short and specific. Under “Work completed” list concrete outputs, such as code, datasets or chapters. Under “Challenges” say what help you need. Link the report to a milestone where you can.

Using publicly available web data

Published 14 Dec 2026

If you collect data from websites, check the site's terms of use, store only what you need, and describe your collection method in the methodology chapter. Discuss any personal data with your supervisor first.

Research proposal template

Published 5 Oct 2026

The required structure for the proposal: problem, objectives, related work, methodology, evaluation plan and timeline. Maximum 8 pages.

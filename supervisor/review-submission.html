Student: Tharushi Fernando · Project: Detecting Code-Mixed Sinhala–English Hate Speech on Social Media

Submitted
Submitted

Deadline
Proposal revision · extended to 12 Mar 2027 Hard

Summary

Hate speech on Sri Lankan social media is often written in code-mixed Sinhala and English, including romanised Sinhala ("Singlish"), which existing English-only classifiers handle poorly. This project builds an annotated dataset of 6,000 public Facebook and YouTube comments, defines a three-level annotation scheme (hate, offensive, neither) with an inter-annotator agreement target of κ ≥ 0.6, and compares a fine-tuned XLM-R model with a character n-gram baseline.

Student's notes on this version

Defined "code-mixed" operationally (section 2.1): a comment with at least one Sinhala and one English token. Replaced the binary labels with a three-level scheme and added the annotation guidelines as Appendix A. Reduced the dataset target from 10,000 to 6,000 comments so annotation fits the timeline.

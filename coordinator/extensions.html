Deadlines

Extensions

An extension gives one student a later due date for one deadline. For a hard deadline it reopens submission for that student only. Students request extensions outside the system.

Grant an extension

Must be later than the original due date.

Cancel

* required · the student and their supervisor see the new date

How extensions work

  • Hard deadline: the student can submit until the new date.
  • Soft deadline: the work is marked on time if it arrives before the new date.
  • Overdue flags use the extended date.
  • Every extension is recorded with the date and who granted it.
